## 🎮 About BlockMan GO

> BlockMan GO is a *sandbox* game similar to **Minecraft** and **Roblox**, created by the developers of **BlockMan GO Studio**. Originally launched in *2015*, the main idea was to create a **platform where players could connect and play online**. It was supposed to be a **launcher** and **multiplayer social network**. However, the developers realized they could create something entirely their own and released **BlockMan GO: Blocky Mods** around *2017*.

> After the official launch of *BlockMan GO: Blocky Mods*, the game became very popular. It was known for being a free non-pirated version of *Minecraft*. *BlockMan GO* (also called *BG* or *BMGO*) evolved and grew very quickly. Currently, it has over [*100 million installs on Google Play Store*](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sandboxol.blockymods&hl=pt_BR)

> *BlockMan GO* has a long story. Moments of **joy**, **achievement**, **loss**, and **difficulty** — just like any other game. Today, *BlockMan GO* brings **nostalgia** to its *old players*, because it is going through **a huge decline**. The community was very strong in **2022**, but the developers simply stopped listening to players. Updates rarely happen, and when they do, they instantly bring a nostalgic feeling.

> Now, *BlockMan GO* is in decline. We need more players. As said before, the developers are not giving attention to the community anymore. *BlockMan GO YouTubers* (known as *BGTubers*) have quit. Only a few small ones remain. The game is becoming extremely expensive, and to complete a simple *event*, *you* need to spend **a lot of money**. However, in minigames where *gcubes (the paid in-game currency)* are not required, it is much easier for everyone to play.

> *BlockMan GO* reached its peak during **2021 to 2022**, during the pandemic, when the game was **not p2w (Pay 2 Win)** and everyone could play fairly, without major advantages or disadvantages. All minigames were very well balanced, and even players who never spent a single cent could easily win matches. Today, the reality is different, and that's why the game is facing major **collapse**. Games like *Bed Wars* are already losing players, who are migrating to other games such as *Roblox* and even *Minecraft*.
